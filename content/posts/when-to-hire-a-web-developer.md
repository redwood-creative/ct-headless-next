Don’t have a website and wondering if you should build one yourself or hire a web developer or designer? You came to the right place. This guide will walk you through the steps you need to take to make this decision.

Both options have their pros and cons, but the decision doesn’t have to be a difficult one. The right choice depends on your specific needs, resources, and technical skills.

## When to Hire a Web Developer/Designer

### Complexity of Requirements

Websites can range from simple to highly complex—some even qualify as web applications. A basic site might only need a contact form, while features like e-commerce, booking systems, member logins, or custom forms add significant complexity.

Platforms like [GoDaddy](https://www.godaddy.com/websites/website-builder "GoDaddy"), [WordPress.com](https://automattic.pxf.io/c/5823154/2101730/22744 "WordPress.com"), [Squarespace](https://squarespace.com/), and [Wix](https://www.wix.com/) can provide good-looking websites with minimal design work or technical skills. But for a truly custom design, hiring a developer or designer can bring your vision to life and ensure a functional experience for your visitors.

[![WordPress.com](//a.impactradius-go.com/display-ad/22744-1910629)](https://automattic.pxf.io/c/5823154/1910629/22744)

### Professionalism and Branding

If maintaining professional branding and consistency across your website is important, hiring a professional can be highly valuable. Many web developers and designers also have branding expertise, or they collaborate with branding and marketing professionals.

A professional can make sure your site aligns with your brand and maintains visual consistency, giving it a polished, credible appearance that builds trust with visitors.

### SEO and Performance Optimization

If optimizing your site for organic reach (unpaid marketing) is a priority, a web developer can help set up basic SEO. For more advanced SEO, you might want to consult an SEO expert.

Website performance also matters. WordPress sites, for example, can become slow if too many plugins are used or if they aren’t maintained. A professional can optimize your site for speed, creating a smoother user experience and improving visitor retention.

### Scalability and Future-Proofing

Do you expect your business to grow or need your website to scale? DIY approaches often limit future expansion. For instance, if you start on a platform like Wix but later want a custom site, you’ll need to rebuild it entirely, which can impact your search engine ranking and other factors.

A developer can build a scalable site that accommodates new features and increased traffic. They can also handle ongoing maintenance, like troubleshooting downtime or updating technologies. If you’re not comfortable maintaining the site yourself, consider hiring help.

### Time and Resource Constraints

Building a website can be time-consuming. While templates speed things up, they tend to be generic and limited in features and branding. Hiring a professional allows you to focus on your core business while leaving the technical work to experts.

If you lack the technical skills, you might spend too much time learning and tinkering with your site instead of running your business. Delegate the technical tasks to those who specialize in them.

## When to Build Your Own Website

### Simplicity of Requirements

If you only need a basic, straightforward website, platforms like Wix, Squarespace, or WordPress may suffice. These platforms offer pre-designed templates and themes that require little technical skill to customize. If you’re okay with a more generic look, you may not need professional assistance.

### Budget Constraints

Building your own website can be more cost-effective, especially if you don’t plan to expand or scale it. By doing it yourself, you save on developer or designer fees.

### Learning and Control

If you’re interested in learning and have the time, building your own website can be a valuable and enjoyable experience. It can also be useful for managing your site as your business grows.

Building your site yourself also gives you full control over the design and timeline. If you’re technically inclined and want hands-on control, a DIY approach might be the right choice.

### Immediate Launch

DIY website builders let you quickly set up and launch a site. This is ideal if you need an online presence right away and don’t plan to scale the site much in the future.

These platforms are designed to be user-friendly, offering drag-and-drop interfaces and step-by-step guides that make the process simple.

## In Summary

Ultimately, whether to hire a developer or build your own website depends on your business’s specific needs, budget, and technical skills.

A DIY website builder can work for simple, budget-friendly sites that don’t need future scalability. However, for more complex, professional, and scalable websites, investing in a professional developer or designer is often the better long-term solution.

Evaluate your business needs carefully and choose the path that aligns with your goals, skills, and resources. The most important thing is to create a functional, visually appealing site that’s easy for users to navigate.
